Please read this first

This is a teaching tool, not financial advice. It shows what the arithmetic of compound interest does if the numbers you type in turn out to be true. Nobody knows what investments will actually return, and real returns arrive unevenly: good years and bad years, not the same percentage every year. No figure on this page is a promise or a guarantee.

It also leaves things out: taxes, fees, health costs, Social Security, pensions, and the order in which good and bad years happen. Use it to understand the shape of the problem, then talk to a qualified adviser before making decisions.
